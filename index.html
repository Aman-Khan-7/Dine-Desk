How the monthly deficit works

1

Students pay an advance daily rate for a set number of days.

2

At month end, the office totals actual kitchen spend for that month.

3

If real cost per day was higher, the shortfall is billed as a deficit.

4

Students clear the deficit online, same as a regular payment.
